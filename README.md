TT Mail Triage Privacy Policy
This app is created and operated solely by Terry Teh for personal email management.

Data Access
The app requests read/write/send access to Gmail via Google OAuth to scan, label, archive, and delete messages. No data is stored externally, transmitted to third parties, or used for any purpose beyond local triage classification.

Data Retention
No email content is persisted outside your account. Labels are applied directly to Gmail within your inbox.
